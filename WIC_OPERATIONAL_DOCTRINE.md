## 1. MISSION STATEMENT

WinterGate Intelligence Collective (WIC) exists to **expose, confront, and dismantle** cyber threats, fraudulent enterprises, and malicious actors that operate with impunity. We do not hack for profit. We do not engage in illegal activity. We document, we expose, and we empower the public to take action.

**Core Principles:**
- **Transparency** – Everything we do is documented and made public
- **Accountability** – Bad actors are held responsible for their actions
- **Legitimacy** – We operate within legal frameworks
- **Empowerment** – We give the public the tools to protect themselves

## 2. OUR OPERATIONAL MODEL

WinterGate IC operates on a **multi-vector engagement model**. We do not rely on a single attack vector. Instead, we engage targets across five simultaneous fronts:

| Front | Method | Example |
|-------|--------|---------|
| **Technical** | Infrastructure mapping, vulnerability discovery, security analysis | Exposing origin IPs, mapping subdomains |
| **Legal** | Regulatory complaints, legal notices, policy violations | FTC complaints, BBB reports, OFAC notices |
| **Public Relations** | Social media campaigns, public dossiers, journalist engagement | Infosec.exchange posts, GitHub repositories |
| **Psychological** | Factual, relentless documentation; calm, persistent pressure | The "Ghost" persona, public timelines |
| **Intelligence** | Continuous monitoring, pattern analysis, threat tracking | GateKeeper network, OSINT bot |

**No single front wins the engagement. The combination of all five forces the target to confront their failures on multiple levels simultaneously.**

## 4. OUR ENGAGEMENT PROCESS – HOW WE CONFRONT BIGGER SERVICES

### Phase 0: Intelligence & Infrastructure
- Build public-facing platforms (wintergate.org)
- Maintain private operational hubs (Discord)
- Create public knowledge repositories (GitHub)
- Establish community of trusted operators (Covenant)

**Goal:** Be ready to act, document, and distribute intelligence at scale.

### Phase 1: Reconnaissance & Discovery
- Identify target infrastructure (servers, providers, IPs)
- Map target's upstream relationships (who hosts them, who protects them)
- Discover vulnerabilities (technical, legal, PR)

**Goal:** Understand the target better than they understand themselves.

### Phase 2: Technical Exploitation & Disclosure
- Exploit vulnerabilities
- Document everything with proof
- Publish findings publicly (GitHub, social media)

**Goal:** Turn a technical discovery into a permanent, public record.

### Phase 3: Multi-Vector Offensive
- **Legal:** File complaints with regulators
- **PR:** Publish findings on multiple platforms
- **Psychological:** Maintain calm, factual, relentless pressure
- **Intelligence:** Monitor target's response, adapt strategy

**Goal:** Force the target to confront their failures on all fronts.

### Phase 4: Escalation & Documentation
- If target is unresponsive: escalate
  - Formal legal notices (criminal liability, regulatory violations)
  - Policy violation notices
  - Public timelines of inaction
- Document every step

**Goal:** Make the target's failure to act more costly than compliance.

### Phase 5: Institutionalization & Infrastructure
- Build permanent tools from operational lessons
  - GateKeeper blacklist network
  - OSINT tools
  - Threat databases
- Strengthen community through training and shared intelligence

**Goal:** Ensure the operation produces lasting value for the community.
