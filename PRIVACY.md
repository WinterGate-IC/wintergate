# WinterGate IC — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: June 2026**

---

## Our Stance on Privacy

WinterGate Intelligence Collective believes privacy is a fundamental right. Our operations are designed to minimize data collection, maximize transparency, and never target ordinary users.

---

## What This Policy Covers

This policy covers data collected through:
- **wintergate.org** (website and GateKeeper dashboard)
- **WinterGate Lite honeypot** (port 8899)
- **Deep Sentinel** (system log monitoring)
- **GateKeeper Discord bot** (threat intelligence platform)

---

## Website Visitors

- **No tracking**: wintergate.org sets no cookies, runs no analytics scripts, and performs no browser fingerprinting
- **No logging of normal visitors**: standard HTTP access logs exist only for operational purposes (error debugging) and are not analyzed for visitor behavior
- **No personal data collected through the website**: the site is purely informational

---

## Threat Data Collection

See our [Data Handling Sheet](DATA-HANDLING.md) for detailed information about:
- What attacker data is collected from honeypots

---

## Data Sharing

- **No data is sold or rented to anyone**
- **No data is shared with third parties for marketing or advertising**
- Aggregated, anonymized statistics (e.g., total tracked users) are publicly available
- Individual threat data is shared only within the collective for operational purposes
- Law enforcement requests through proper legal channels will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis

---

## Your Rights

If your data is in our systems:
1. **Right to know**: contact us to learn what data we hold
2. **Right to deletion**: request removal of your identifiers
3. **Right to rectification**: request correction of inaccurate data
